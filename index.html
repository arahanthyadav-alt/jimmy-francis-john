About

The Sound of
the Soul

Jimmy Francis John is an Indian indie artist and vocalist who weaves together the rich tapestry of South Indian musical traditions with contemporary indie-folk sensibilities. As a key voice of the Bengaluru-based progressive fusion band Pineapple Express, he has been at the forefront of India's evolving independent music scene.

His solo work transcends language barriers — singing in Tamil, Malayalam, Hindi, and beyond — creating intimate, emotionally resonant soundscapes that feel both ancient and modern. From the moonlit reverie of "Nila" to the tender warmth of "Kadhali", every song is a journey inward.
